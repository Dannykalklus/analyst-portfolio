My MSc research. Does the cost of complying with GRA tax obligations hurt small business performance, and does responsibility accounting soften the damage? Survey of 384 SMEs in Greater Accra, analysed with PLS-SEM and 5,000 bootstrap resamples.

What it means

For SME owners: compliance cost is a real drag on profitability and sales (a 1 SD rise lowers outcomes by 0.29 SD). Firms that assign clear cost responsibility, budget by unit and hold managers to account lose much less: at high responsibility accounting the effect almost disappears.

For GRA and policymakers: simpler filing and clearer guidance would ease the burden directly. Training SMEs in basic management accounting would also help them absorb the burden that remains.

Limitations: cross-sectional, self-reported data from one region; purposive sampling limits generalisation; outcomes are perceived rather than audited figures.

Survey data collected by the author through Google Forms (29 Sep to 16 Oct 2026), 384 valid responses, no straight-lining detected. PLS-SEM implemented in Python (Lohmöller algorithm, path weighting, two-stage interaction; Hair et al., 2022 criteria). Raw responses are kept private to protect respondents. Daniel N. T.
