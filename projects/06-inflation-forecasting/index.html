Inflation history and forecast

Year-on-year CPI inflation, %. Forecast from the ARDL model, with 80% and 95% bands from 1,500 bootstrapped simulations

The central path keeps inflation close to 5% through October 2027, below the Bank of Ghana's 8% ± 2% medium-term target band. The bands are wide because they carry the volatility of the past 25 years, including the 54% peak in December 2022. The SARIMA model (dotted) drifts up toward the 25-year average, which is why it does worse when the regime changes.

Forecast accuracy by horizon

Root mean squared error, percentage points, 82 forecast origins (Jan 2019 to Oct 2025)

Both models beat the random walk at every horizon. Adding the exchange rate (ARDL) gives the lowest error at 3, 6 and 12 months, cutting 12-month error by 14%.

The cedi leads inflation

Year-on-year inflation vs year-on-year depreciation of the cedi against the dollar, %

The 2022 currency collapse fed directly into the inflation spike. The 2025 appreciation drove the fall to 3%.

How fast the cedi reaches prices

Cumulative effect of a 1% depreciation on monthly inflation, by months since the shock

About 17% passes through directly within a year (jointly significant, p = 0.001). Including the persistence of inflation itself, the long-run pass-through is 0.68.

What it means

  • Base case: inflation stays around 4.3 to 6.0% over the next year. That leaves room for the Bank of Ghana to hold or cut its 14% policy rate, and real interest rates stay high.
  • Main risk is the cedi: a 15% depreciation would lift inflation to about 8.0% by Oct 2027, back to the middle of the target band.
  • For valuation work: this path supports using a long-run inflation assumption of about 5 to 8% in cedi discount rates, as in my MTN Ghana model.

Method

Monthly CPI (not seasonally adjusted) and GHS/USD from the World Bank Global Economic Monitor, January 2000 to July 2026, extended to October 2026 with Ghana Statistical Service year-on-year rates. The models work on monthly log changes. SARIMA(1,0,1)(1,0,1)12 uses its own history only. The ARDL model uses inflation lags 1, 2 and 12 plus six lags of cedi depreciation, with the exchange rate held flat in forecasts. The rolling-origin backtest re-estimates every model at each origin. Pass-through is estimated by OLS with HAC standard errors over 2003-01 to 2026-10.
