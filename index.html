MTN Ghana (Scancom PLC): what is it worth?

A driver-based Excel model built from the FY2025 audited statements, valued with a free-cash-flow-to-equity DCF and a dividend discount model.

8.42Blended fair value, GHS
6.67GSE price, GHS
+26%Implied upside

Findings

  • Data now drives the business. It made up 55% of service revenue in FY2025, up from 50%, after growing 48.8%. Voice fell to 16%.
  • Margins are unusually high. EBITDA margin was 60.1% and return on equity 63%.
  • The balance sheet holds net cash even after counting GHS 3.6bn of lease liabilities as debt.
  • I stripped out the GHS 38.4bn Mobile Money float, which is customer money matched by e-money obligations. Left in, it distorts liquidity and return ratios.
  • The answer depends on the discount rate. Terminal value is 64% of equity value, so the sensitivity grid is part of the result.

Project pipeline

Six projects on Ghanaian companies, markets and finance operations. Each one comes with its data, method and a written conclusion.

  1. 01

    MTN Ghana equity valuation

    Audited statements, ratio analysis, five-year forecast, DCF and DDM, sensitivity analysis.

    Complete
  2. 02

    Ghana macro dashboard

    Inflation, the policy rate, the cedi and the external position from World Bank, GSS and Bank of Ghana data, with a Python pipeline that refreshes it. Finding: the real policy rate rose from 3.5 to 14.3 points while the Bank cut by 1,400 bp.

    Complete
  3. 03

    Accounts payable audit analytics

    Benford's Law, duplicate-payment and concentration tests on all 115,797 City of Chicago vendor payments in 2025 ($10.66bn). Flagged 832 possible duplicate pairs ($146.6m) and missing department coding on 54% of lines.

    Complete
  4. 04

    Tax compliance cost and SME performance

    My MSc research: PLS-SEM on a survey of 384 Accra SMEs. Compliance cost lowers performance (β −0.29), and responsibility accounting cuts that effect from −0.50 to −0.09.

    Complete
  5. 05

    Credit default risk model

    Logistic regression vs gradient boosting on 30,000 real credit card accounts (AUC 0.78, Gini 0.56). Declining the riskiest 20% would cut the approved bad rate from 22.1% to 13.6%.

    Complete
  6. 06

    Inflation and exchange rate forecasting

    Time-series models for Ghana CPI and USD/GHS, tested against a simple benchmark.

    Planned
